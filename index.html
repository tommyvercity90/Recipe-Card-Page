Chocolate Cake

A soft, moist, and delicious chocolate cake that's perfect for birthdays and special occasions.

Prep Time 20 min
Cook Time 35 min
Servings 8

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Preparation

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Grease and flour a round cake pan.
  3. Mix flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, and baking soda in a bowl.
  4. Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla extract. Mix until smooth.
  5. Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan.
  6. Bake for 30–35 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  7. Allow the cake to cool before serving.
